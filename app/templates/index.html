Internship Portal

Students build a profile skills, course, CGPA and get matched against open roles. Employers post jobs and review applicants ranked by relevance instead of sorting through everything by hand.

Sign up as a student Sign up as an employer
How the matching works

A hybrid recommendation engine does the ranking: content-based filtering compares a student's listed skills against each job's requirements, and collaborative filtering looks at what similar students have applied to or shown interest in. A student who's new and doesn't have much activity yet falls back to content-based matching alone until there's enough data to do more.

What's built in
  • Student and employer accounts with role-based dashboards
  • Job posting, browsing, and search with filters
  • CV upload for students
  • Application tracking (pending, reviewed, shortlisted, rejected)
  • Admin view for user and job moderation
