About this project

About Internship Portal

Internship Portal is a web-based internship and recruitment platform built to connect students with internship and job opportunities that actually fit their skills, course of study, and interests — instead of leaving them to scroll through generic listings. This project was built as a final-year capstone project, covering the full stack: database design, backend APIs, authentication and role-based access control, and the matching algorithm itself.
